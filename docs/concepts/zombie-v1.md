# Концепт зомби · v1

Дата: 2026-10-16. Создано по запросу пользователя встроенным **ImageGen**,
без CLI/API fallback. [Изображение](zombie-v1.png) скопировано в проект без
растровой правки; оригинал генератора сохранён.

Это художественный вариант для обсуждения, не утверждённая замена игровой
модели и не готовый спрайт/атлас анимаций. Игровые ресурсы и код не изменены.
Военно-исторический образ выбран как продолжение сохранённого направления
разрушенного вокзала из `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md`; пользователь не задавал
для этого концепта конкретную форму, национальность или новый канон.

Лист: общий вид заражённого пехотинца, крупный план головы и профиль для
обсуждения будущей боковой анимации. При просмотре проверены полный рост,
читаемость силуэта, две руки/две ноги, согласованность одежды и отсутствие
интерфейса/подписей. Лист не проверялся как игровой ассет.

## Исходный промпт

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original zombie enemy character concept sheet for the 2D side-scrolling game "Мёртвый перрон" (Dead Platform).
Primary request: a polished, cohesive concept art sheet exploring ONE ordinary undead infantryman for a ruined European railway station setting inspired by the mid-1940s.
Style/medium: sophisticated hand-painted semi-realistic 2D game character design, confident expressive dark contours, economical painterly texture, large readable shapes and restrained material detail. Visually compelling production concept art, not pixel art, not photorealism, not a 3D render, not a game screenshot.
Subject: a gaunt, stooped adult undead infantryman, slightly forward head and rounded shoulders, thin grey-green ashen face, sunken dull amber eyes, slack asymmetrical mouth, a dented tilted olive steel helmet, weathered grey-olive military tunic with torn cuffs and one loose flap, faded canvas shoulder strap and belt with a small pouch, worn dark trousers with frayed knees, mismatched worn heavy leather boots. No weapon. Two distinct arms and two intact legs, fingers slightly curled, one hand reaching while the other hangs lower; believable anatomy and weight, clear shoulder/elbow/wrist and hip/knee/ankle articulation for later side-view segmented animation. Undead appearance through posture, skin and expression, minimal dried stains, no exposed entrails or graphic gore. No national or ideological insignia.
Composition/framing: wide landscape character design presentation, approximately 16:9. A large full-body three-quarter standing/shambling character on the left, entirely visible with breathing room above helmet and below boots. On the right a somewhat smaller FULL-BODY strict side profile facing right of exactly the SAME character with matching costume, helmet, face and proportions, in a readable shambling step; above it a head-and-shoulders close-up of the same character. These are consistent studies of ONE enemy design, not a lineup of different enemies. Flat quiet smoky light slate-grey paper background with subtle brush texture and just soft grounding shadows; no scenery, no floor props, no panels or frames.
Lighting/mood: cold overcast station daylight, soft directional illumination, crisp silhouette, melancholy threatening human character rather than a monstrous superhero. Readable midtones. A very restrained warm edge accent consistent with distant station fires.
Color palette: muted olive, desaturated cold blue-grey, charcoal, weathered brown leather, pallid grey-green skin; tiny subdued amber eye accents, no neon.
Constraints: no text, no labels, no letters, no watermark, no logos, no UI, no gun, no extra limbs, no duplicated hands, no cropped feet. Strong elegant presentation and character identity.
```
